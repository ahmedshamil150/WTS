Ask our team →
Which ultrasound machine is right for my practice?

Tell us about your specialty, room size, workflow, and budget. We will help compare portable and cart-based systems that fit your day-to-day work.

Can you match a probe to my existing machine?

Yes. Share the make and model of your system, or send us the part number, and we will confirm compatibility before you order.

Do you sell replacement parts and accessories?

Yes. We source batteries, cables, boards, gel, and other ultrasound accessories for a range of systems.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Most requests receive a clear response within 24 hours. Include the equipment or part details you have so we can move quickly.
