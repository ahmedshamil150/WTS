The small parts that
keep a system moving.

Cables, batteries, boards and consumables — the low-cost items that decide whether a room stays booked or goes dark.

Equipment care4 min read09 Sep 2026

When an ultrasound room goes down, the cause is rarely dramatic. More often it is a cable that has been flexed one time too many, a battery that no longer holds charge, or a box of gel that ran out on a busy Monday. The small parts decide whether a room stays booked.

Cables and connectors

Probe cables take the most punishment of any part of the system — coiled, pulled, clamped and disinfected every day. Strain at the connector end and wear along the length are the usual failure points. Replacing a cable early is almost always cheaper than discovering the fault mid-exam, and matching the exact connector matters just as much as it does for a replacement probe.

Batteries and power

Portable systems live and die by their batteries. A pack that no longer holds a usable charge turns a mobile cart into a tethered one, and a failing power supply shows up as random shutdowns that look like software problems. Both are straightforward to source once the model and part number are known.

Boards and modules

Mainboards, interface boards and I/O modules are where sourcing gets specific. Part numbers matter here more than anywhere else — revisions are not interchangeable, and a board from a closely related model can still be the wrong board. Send the number off the part itself rather than relying on the system name alone.

Consumables nobody notices

Gel, probe covers, printer media, keyboards, carts and covers do not fail so much as run out. They are the avoidable delays: a scan delayed because the covers are in another building. Keeping a short reorder list with your machine model attached makes restocking a one-line request.

Keep a one-page list of your machines, probes and the parts you reorder most. It turns a half-hour of searching into a single message.

Whatever the part, the same four details speed everything up: machine model, part number, quantity and timing.
